6 Common Questions About Payout Annuities

Q1. Can I change my mind or cancel after purchasing?

As stated in the contract disclaimer, once annuity payouts are locked in, they are completely irrevocable, and legislative regulations may affect the types you can purchase. Therefore, never convert all of your savings into an annuity at once; you must retain emergency liquidity reserves. An advisor will help determine how much to convert based on your total assets and monthly budget shortfall.

Q2. What if I pass away shortly after buying? Does my money disappear?

Not necessarily, depending on whether you add a guaranteed period. A life annuity can include a 10- or 15-year guaranteed period rider. If death occurs within the guaranteed period, beneficiaries receive remaining scheduled payments or a lump-sum death benefit according to funding source and contract terms. After the guaranteed period ends, no death benefit remains, and adding a guarantee period will lower the monthly payout amount—exact trade-offs must be evaluated via official quotes rather than assuming principal is unconditionally preserved.

Q3. Are monthly payments guaranteed never to decrease?

It depends on the selected income mode. Level income stays constant each month; indexed income increases annually by a specified percentage (e.g., 2%); integrated income intentionally reduces the annuity payout portion once government benefits commence so total overall cash flow remains balanced. Saying "payments never decrease" is not a universal rule; your choice depends on whether you fear inflation more or monthly income adjustments.

Q4. What about inflation? Will fixed payouts be enough in 20 years?

Fixed payments experience purchasing power erosion over time. An indexed income option scales payouts annually to help offset inflation, but initial starting payouts will be lower. The exact spread is determined in the carrier quote. It should not be framed as guaranteed to outpace inflation, nor that level income will definitely fall short—compare both against your anticipated living expense growth.

Q5. Is it wise to buy at current interest rates? What if rates rise later?

Monthly payout rates depend on age, gender, premium volume, and prevailing market interest rates at issue. Payout rates lock in at contract signing; you cannot retroactively reset your contract if rates rise in the future. Understand this trade-off clearly before committing rather than assuming adjustments are possible later.

Q6. How is taxation handled? Are RRSP, RRIF, TFSA, and Non-Registered treated the same?

No, tax treatment differs fundamentally by capital source. Net after-tax cash flow depends entirely on your account structure. After-tax illustrations provided by advisors are for demonstrative comparison only and do not constitute formal tax advice. Actual tax liabilities must be reviewed and finalized by a licensed professional accountant based on your personal financial profile.

Live by Your Rules, Custom-Tailored for Your Needs

Everyone has unique retirement visions, but nobody wants to stress about base living bills. We can build custom extraction strategies utilizing your RRSP, RRIF, TFSA, or non-registered capital structures.

1. Select Your Annuity Life Cycle

Single Life Annuity

Provides guaranteed payouts for your entire life. Payouts persist for as long as you live, even past age 110, completely eradicating the risk of outliving your capital reserves.

Joint Life Annuity

Guarantees payouts for two lives. Extremely ideal for spouses; income continues seamlessly until the last surviving spouse passes away.

Term Certain Annuity

Secures guaranteed payouts for a designated term (e.g. 10 or 20 years). If you pass away during this term, all remaining payments flow directly to your designated beneficiaries.

2. Configure Income Growth Patterns

  • ✓

    Level Payouts

    Income levels remain exactly constant across time, enabling absolute budget precision.

  • ✓

    Indexed Payouts

    Payments scale upward annually by a fixed percentage (e.g. 2% or 3%) to fight long-term inflation erosion.

  • ✓

    Integrated Payouts

    For early retirees. Payouts start higher to bridge the gap before government pensions (OAS/CPP) commence, then decrease slightly afterward to preserve a flat aggregate cash flow profile.

"What if I pass away shortly after purchasing an annuity?"

Understanding the Guaranteed Period Rider

This is a very common concern. To mitigate this scenario, you can easily attach a **"Guaranteed Period"** rider (e.g. 10 or 15 years) to your Single or Joint Life annuity contract.

If you pass away before the guaranteed period ends, the insurance carrier will **never** retain your capital! We continue monthly disbursements to your designated beneficiaries, or pay out the remaining discounted value as a lump-sum death benefit.

💡 Enjoy guaranteed lifetime cash flow while securing designated protection for early passing. Adding a guaranteed period will lower monthly payouts—see quote for exact figures.
