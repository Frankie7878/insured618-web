Clarifying Doubts

Top 6 Questions & Common Concerns About Segregated Funds

Q1. Is principal guaranteed at all times? Can I withdraw immediately during a drop and get 100% back?

No. The contractual guarantee of 75% to 100% (net of prior withdrawals) applies only upon contract maturity or death, subject to required holding periods. If you withdraw mid-term, the payout is calculated at prevailing market value where guarantees do not apply. Specific maturity dates and conditions are governed by individual contracts; it must not be mistaken for "daily capital protection."

Q2. How should I choose between 75/75, 75/100, and 100/100 tiers?

These tiers reflect distinct priorities: Investment Class offers 75% maturity and 75% death protection; Estate Class offers 75% maturity and 100% death protection (frequently featured on comparison pages); Protection Class provides 100% maturity and 100% death protection. Your choice depends on whether maturity preservation or legacy transfer matters more. Higher guarantees affect fund expense ratios (MERs) and restrictions in the fund facts—decisions should align with personalized illustrations rather than popularity badges.

Q3. Can the reset feature automatically lock in profits every year as a new guarantee floor?

Contracts generally allow policyholders to elect resets (commonly up to age 80) to lock market appreciation into a higher guaranteed principal base. However, reset frequencies, age caps, and whether resetting extends the maturity timeframe depend strictly on individual contract terms. Resets require meeting specific timeline rules and are not unconditional automatic annual locks.

Q4. Does buying a segregated fund grant automatic and complete immunity from creditors?

No. Contracts state that assets "may be protected from creditor claims" when qualified family members are designated as beneficiaries. However, which relationships qualify and how rules apply depend on provincial legislation and contract terms, and protection never shields fraudulent conveyances made to evade existing debts. Always seek qualified legal advice rather than assuming automatic, universal immunity.

Q5. Is probate truly bypassed completely, and are the cost savings guaranteed?

It depends on beneficiary designation. Death benefits are paid directly, swiftly, and privately to designated beneficiaries, bypassing provincial probate—provided the beneficiary is not the estate itself. Settlement procedures follow provincial law and policy terms. Any numerical case comparisons seen online are purely illustrative examples; they do not represent guaranteed fixed dollar savings for every household.

Q6. Is it really zero penalty with every dollar working in the market?

It is not zero cost. The cost of guarantee protection is embedded within the fund's Management Expense Ratio (MER). The "chargeback" model means early redemption charges are absorbed by the advisor rather than deducted from client accounts, not that the investment carries zero management expense. Furthermore, F-Class or preferred pricing tiers require minimum investments of $300k to $500k+. Specific fee rates must be verified in the Fund Facts for your target fund.
